Granules and CSV output

No collections selected yet.
Satellite + ground rows stream into one model-ready CSV OpenAQ supplies ground air-monitor observations when a key is provided. NOAA/NCEI supplies mapped weather-station observations. OpenStreetMap supplies ground/reference infrastructure features. Components without a scientifically defensible public ground equivalent are marked unavailable rather than assigned invented values.
All matching granules from every selected collection are collected Granule discovery and conversion use adaptive parallel workers with a low-concurrency recovery pass. Harmony-compatible products are subset by NASA before transfer whenever possible.
CSV schema is model-ready and explicitly segmented Rows include component segment, collection ID/short name/title/version/provider, granule identity, satellite/instrument, UTC timestamp + date + time, data cycle, latitude/longitude, variable, value, unit, weight fields when supplied by the product, and preserved extra product attributes.

Strict clean scope is recommended for Lupus Cortex training. The converter may inspect extra source variables internally during recovery, but only selected-component rows inside the requested UTC date range are permitted into the final CSV. On supported desktop browsers, accepted rows are appended as soon as they finish.
