Granules and CSV output

No collections selected yet.
All matching granules from every selected collection are collected Granule discovery and conversion use adaptive parallel workers with a low-concurrency recovery pass. Harmony-compatible products are subset by NASA before transfer whenever possible.
CSV schema is model-ready and explicitly segmented Rows include component segment, collection ID/short name/title/version/provider, granule identity, satellite/instrument, UTC timestamp + date + time, data cycle, latitude/longitude, variable, value, unit, weight fields when supplied by the product, and preserved extra product attributes.

Low-Bandwidth Training Mode is recommended for Lupus Cortex training. It preserves component/collection/time/space/variable provenance and aggregates numeric samples into spatial cells with weighted mean, mean, standard deviation, min, max, and sample count. Raw-row mode preserves every converted row but necessarily transfers more data.
