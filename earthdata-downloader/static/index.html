Granules and CSV output

All matching granules are collected The downloader reads every CMR result page. During CSV creation, granules are converted one-by-one in serverless-safe chunks and merged into one CSV in your browser.
CSV time metadata is explicit Every output includes UTC data timestamp, timestamp source, granule start/end, retrieval time, data cycle, cycle interval in seconds, and a readable cycle detail such as “Hourly · this row: 14:00:00 UTC”.

The search has no artificial collection or granule limit. Large jobs are split into one-granule conversion requests automatically to reduce serverless timeout and response-size failures. A single exceptionally large granule may still require a variable filter, smaller boundary box, or row limit.
